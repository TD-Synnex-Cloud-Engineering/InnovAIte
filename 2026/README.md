# Microsoft InnovAIte Symposium 2026

The 2026 Microsoft InnovAIte Symposium moved to the Loews Royal Pacific Resort at Universal Orlando and was the first InnovAIte to include a dedicated technical program alongside the business and partner tracks. General keynotes, breakout sessions, and technical tracks covered agentic AI, Microsoft Copilot, security, cloud, and device modernization.

## Highlights

- Technical, business, and partner tracks, plus general keynote sessions.
- Technical sessions held Monday, February 23, 2026, delivered by TD SYNNEX Cloud Engineering and Microsoft.
- The TD SYNNEX Microsoft Cloud Technical Series scheduled its February 23, 2026 session as "Live from InnovAIte."
- **ISV InnovAItion Studio**, where ISV and alliance partners showcased solutions.
- Offsite evening event at Universal Orlando on Tuesday, February 24, with group transportation.
- Registration and badge pick-up opened Monday, February 23 at 10:00 a.m. in the Pre-Function West Foyer.
- Session calendars and onsite updates were provided through the TD SYNNEX Events app.
