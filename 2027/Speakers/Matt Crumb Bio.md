# Matt Crumb

**Solution Architect, Cloud Engineering, Azure, TD SYNNEX**

He began his IT career on a help desk in 1999 and has spent most of it in presales engineering, including 18 years with a financial technology company and a role as a Microsoft contractor supporting the Windows Server business in distribution. Matt joined TD SYNNEX in 2021 to drive Azure Stack HCI (now Azure Local) opportunities before moving into Azure presales engineering.
