# Microsoft InnovAIte Symposium 2027

The 2027 Microsoft InnovAIte Symposium brings Microsoft and TD SYNNEX together with partners for four days of strategy, innovation, technical learning, and business growth at Disney's BoardWalk Inn. The program covers Microsoft's biggest opportunities across AI, Copilot, cloud, security, data, business applications, and devices, with the goal of each partner leaving with a practical Microsoft growth plan for 2027.

## Highlights

- Microsoft and TD SYNNEX keynote sessions.
- Business growth and profitability discussions: Microsoft growth strategy, high-growth solution areas, incentives, and customer adoption and retention.
- Technical breakouts and hands-on labs.
- Practice-building workshops and Innovation Studio solution showcases.
- Solution and program announcements, and networking with Microsoft-focused partners.
- **Thursday, February 4 — optional workshops** (attendees select one track):
  - *Technical Workshop Track* (one session from each block) — Block 1: *Unifying On-Premises and Cloud: Practical Azure Arc Implementation* or *Building a Resilient Cloud: Master Azure Security*; Block 2: *Architecting Success: Building Azure Environments with WAF & CAF* or *From Legacy to Leading Edge: Modernizing Data with Microsoft Fabric*.
  - *Customer Zero Workshop* — an interactive Microsoft Copilot business strategy session (two attendees per company: one technical or practice lead and one business lead) covering Copilot maturity assessment, adoption strategy, customer workshops, pricing, and a 30/60/90-day action plan.
