# Microsoft InnovAIte Symposium 2025

The 2nd annual Microsoft InnovAIte Symposium returned to Orlando, Florida, bringing TD SYNNEX customers and Microsoft partners together to explore Microsoft's latest AI capabilities and how to turn them into practice growth. TD SYNNEX and Microsoft subject matter experts and leaders led sessions on profitability, solutions, sales strategy, and devices.

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Edition** | 2nd annual |
| **Dates** | Monday, March 31 – Thursday, April 3, 2025 |
| **Venue** | Loews Portofino Bay Hotel at Universal Orlando |
| **Location** | 5601 Universal Boulevard, Orlando, Florida 32819 |
| **Hosts** | TD SYNNEX and Microsoft |
| **Audience** | TD SYNNEX Microsoft partners and customers |
| **Status** | Completed |

## Highlights

- Maximizing profitability through Microsoft programs and incentives, including the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program.
- Solution sessions across Microsoft Cloud, Security, Data, and AI.
- Refining sales strategy and uncovering upsell opportunities.
- The AI PC and device refresh opportunity, including AI PC readiness with Microsoft 365 Copilot.
- New for 2025: the **Microsoft InnovAItion Lab**, featuring Microsoft alliance vendors and strategic ISV partners.
- Evening partner celebration at The Wizarding World of Harry Potter at Universal Studios.
- Registration and badge pick-up opened Monday, March 31 at 10:00 a.m. in the Tuscan Foyer (lower level).
- Business-focused program; there were no technical sessions in 2025.
